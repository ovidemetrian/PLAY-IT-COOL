# Möbius Bar Mini Player

Your own songs from the phone, a live glowing wave and the Möbius Bar lights, and a one-tap video of the song for YouTube, on one square stage that looks the same on a phone and a computer.

**Open it:** https://ovidemetrian.github.io/PLAY-IT-COOL/ (then **Install** or **Add to home screen**)

## What it does

- **Opens empty:** add songs from your phone; they stay in the player for next time, on your phone only.
- **Play:** a live wave edge to edge, the song's cover riding the timeline between two colour-changing time bubbles, the title, artist and album scrolling under the line, and five glass buttons: repeat, previous, play, next and the Möbius eye for full screen.
- **Twelve looks** that change on their own: Ovi's colours, lava, disco, dark red, neon, candle, ocean, sunset, fire, galaxy, ice and jungle.
- **Show mode:** the wave goes huge, the picture floats with the running time, and MADE BY OVI becomes a paddle to bounce it back.
- **Video:** save the whole song as a horizontal (16:9) or vertical (9:16, Shorts) video, ready for YouTube.
- **About page:** tap **MADE BY OVI**.

The big player lives at https://ovidemetrian.github.io/MOBIUS-BAR-PLAY-THE-LAMP/

After changing files, raise `CACHE` in `sw.js` so installed copies update.
